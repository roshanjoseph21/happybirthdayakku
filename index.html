Hey Akku! ❤️

So, it's your day! The day the world got one of its most beautiful souls, and I got a cousin who would eventually become one of my favourite people.

I hope this year brings you adventures, laughter, unexpected blessings, and prayers answered. And when life gets busy, let's still make time for our random conversations and our usual nonsense.

Let's keep growing up without growing apart. There are still so many memories waiting for us. ❤️

Here's to you, Akku — my five-days-younger partner in this lifetime. Keep being wonderful. The world needs more people like you.

Happy Birthday, love you loads!
Roshan ❤️
